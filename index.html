Qamar support

Moon and planet photo planner

Qamar tells you when tonight is worth photographing the Moon and planets, and keeps a journal of your shots. Qamar means "moon" in Arabic.

Get in touch

Found a bug or have an idea? Email dankhan087@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

Qamar can't find my location

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Privacy & Security, then Location Services, find Qamar and choose "While Using the App".

I'm not getting evening alerts

In Qamar's Settings tab, check that evening alerts are on. Then check iPhone Settings, Notifications, Qamar. Reminders are scheduled each time you open the app, so open it every few days to keep them coming.

A planet says it isn't visible

It is either below the horizon, or the sky isn't dark enough yet. Check the planet's detail screen to see when it rises, peaks and sets tonight.

I can't add a photo

Open iPhone Settings, find Qamar, and allow access to Photos and Camera.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries from the Journal tab, or delete the app, which removes everything it stores on your phone. Qamar has no accounts and no servers holding your data.
